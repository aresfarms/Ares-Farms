# CANON-STRIPE-CONNECT-ALLOCATION-001

## Status
Founder-approved architecture; transfer execution remains promotion-inactive.

## Canonical rule
Furlong is the platform payment collector. Approved module-provider allocations may be transferred only to certified, contractually authorized provider accounts under a versioned revenue-class rule. No external broker, former partner, or portal user has an automatic economic entitlement. Any amount not allocated remains in the Furlong Stripe platform balance.

## Required controls
1. Revenue allocation rules are versioned and immutable once used.
2. Percentages are stored as basis points and may vary by revenue class/module.
3. No allocation rule may exceed 100% of the originating payment.
4. The residual amount is always the Furlong-retained amount.
5. No transfer may execute unless the recipient Connected Account is certified and the originating Stripe source transaction is bound.
6. Stripe is an execution provider, not the source of truth for economic entitlement.
7. The canonical allocation evidence record must exist before any external transfer.
8. Historical payments retain the rule version effective for that payment.
9. Refund, dispute, reversal, tax, and accounting treatment require separate governed reconciliation.
10. Until an approved rule exists, the default is retain-all: 100% stays with Furlong.

## Owner-controlled economics overlay
11. No portal user, external professional spoke, former founder, former steward, or former partner receives an automatic module allocation or general-fund distribution.
12. Provider compensation requires a current written agreement, a certified payout destination, an approved revenue-class rule, and conflict-governance review where the provider can influence routing or recommendations.
13. Environmental-module revenue and any future licensed-professional-module revenue are allocated only under the current provider agreement for the work actually performed; absent an approved rule, retain-all applies.
14. All otherwise unallocated current platform revenue enters the Furlong general fund.
15. General-fund revenue first absorbs processor costs, taxes, refunds/chargebacks, reserves, and necessary current operating expenses.
16. Owner/platform stewardship compensation, expense reimbursement, build recovery, and distributions are governed by current corporate approvals and accounting records rather than permanent personal percentages embedded in platform doctrine.
17. No compensation rule may create a hidden incentive to steer a borrower toward an affiliated lender, broker, environmental provider, or other spoke.
18. Any future affiliated lending company is a separate spoke and receives compensation only under disclosed, lawful, arm's-length rules applicable to that transaction.
19. Historical allocation records remain immutable evidence and are not rewritten by this transition.
20. New allocation evidence must identify the revenue class, legal basis, recipient certification, governing rule version, conflict review where applicable, and retained amount before transfer.
21. Professional work paid through a module must not also be added to a common-platform build-recovery ledger unless a separately approved accounting rule expressly permits it.
22. Build-recovery balances and later additions require immutable evidence and an approved valuation methodology; prior periods are never rewritten.
