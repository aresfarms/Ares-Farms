# FURLONG Progressive Intelligence Doctrine v1.0

**Doctrine ID:** PROGRESSIVE-INTELLIGENCE-001  
**Status:** Release-candidate design doctrine  
**Applies to:** Customer property discovery, property analysis, comparison, My Cases, reports, and future outcome tracking

## Core principle

FURLONG should never merely deliver an answer.

Every serious customer surface should make five things legible:

1. what the customer has discovered,
2. what the evidence currently supports,
3. what remains unknown or unresolved,
4. what additional evidence could unlock next,
5. how the customer's accumulated property intelligence is becoming more useful.

The customer reward is not points, streaks, artificial scarcity, or withheld information. The reward is better evidence, better comparisons, better decisions, better execution, and a growing private collection of intelligence.

## Canonical loop

**Find → Reveal → Question → Investigate → Verify → Unlock → Compare → Shortlist → Improve → Measure → Learn → Discover**

A property may move through this loop repeatedly as sources refresh, the customer contributes evidence, the contemplated use changes, financing changes, or actual operating results replace assumptions.

## Evidence maturity

FURLONG may describe the state of evidence using governed states already supported by the platform, including:

- VERIFIED
- SUPPORTED
- SCREENING
- NEEDS EVIDENCE

Customer-facing progress must be derived from real evidence and real workflow state. It must not imply that a property is “80% complete” unless the denominator is itself a governed, explicit checklist with a defensible meaning.

## Ethical engagement constraints

FURLONG MUST NOT:

- withhold a known conclusion merely to make the customer return,
- manufacture uncertainty or fake an “unlock,”
- delay a source-backed fact that is already available,
- create streak pressure, countdown pressure, loot-box mechanics, or false scarcity,
- treat missing evidence as zero,
- convert an unresolved fact into a positive finding,
- imply lender approval, appraisal, permit approval, environmental clearance, or professional certification where none exists,
- rank an alternative merely because the customer prefers it.

FURLONG MAY create anticipation only where the real-world evidence is genuinely incomplete or a source refresh could materially change the result.

## Discovery presentation

The property workspace should expose a compact progressive-intelligence layer near the Furlong Answer.

At minimum it should show:

- **Discovered:** how much source-backed information has actually been assembled.
- **Still unknown:** the highest-value unresolved evidence.
- **Unlock next:** the next useful evidence-gathering action.
- **Opportunity signal:** what appears possible under the current evidence state.
- **Compare:** the ability to place the property beside alternatives without treating missing evidence as zero.
- **Shortlist / collection:** a durable private path for keeping the property investigation.
- **Improve:** a future state where supported assumptions can be changed deliberately and the effect on economics/risk is shown.
- **Measure:** a future state where actual acquisition, renovation, revenue, expense, NOI, financing, and disposition data can replace assumptions.

## Collection doctrine

A customer's intelligence collection should become more valuable even when the customer does not acquire a property.

Useful lifecycle states include:

- discovered,
- investigating,
- shortlisted,
- rejected / passed,
- underwritten,
- acquired,
- improving,
- operating,
- exited.

A rejected property remains useful evidence about what the customer screened out and why.

## Comparison doctrine

Property comparison must remain evidence-safe:

- compare equivalent fields where possible,
- show missing evidence explicitly,
- preserve source dates,
- never convert “unknown” into zero,
- never fabricate a winner,
- allow a customer to promote an investigation into a durable private collection.

## Improvement doctrine

When a supported scenario exists, FURLONG should be able to show the effect of changing one or more supported assumptions, such as:

- acquisition price,
- renovation budget,
- revenue,
- occupancy/utilization,
- operating expenses,
- financing terms,
- use mix,
- environmental or infrastructure mitigation.

The system should distinguish a customer-entered scenario from a source-backed fact.

## Outcome doctrine

The loop does not end at acquisition.

Future outcome tracking should reconcile modeled and actual values for items such as:

- acquisition price,
- capital improvements,
- stabilized revenue,
- operating expenses,
- NOI,
- DSCR,
- occupancy/utilization,
- financing cost,
- disposition proceeds.

Actuals must never silently overwrite the original baseline. FURLONG should preserve the baseline, the actual, the variance, the date, and the source or customer assertion that produced the actual.

## Refresh doctrine

When a governed source refresh changes a material fact, FURLONG should:

1. preserve the prior observation where replay/audit requirements call for it,
2. show the changed fact,
3. identify downstream conclusions that may be affected,
4. recompute only where the evidence contract permits it,
5. surface the change to the customer without manufacturing urgency.

## Product test

A successful property page should make the customer naturally ask:

> “What did FURLONG find, what still matters, and what changes if I learn one more thing?”

That is the desired engagement loop.
