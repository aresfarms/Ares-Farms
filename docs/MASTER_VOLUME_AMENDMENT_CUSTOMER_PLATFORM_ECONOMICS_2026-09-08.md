# Master Volume Series Amendment — Unified Customer Relationship, Neutral Capital Network, and Current Economics

**Amendment ID:** MV-AMD-2026-09-08-CUSTOMER-PLATFORM-ECONOMICS-001  
**Status:** Active governed implementation amendment; incorporated into repository conformance pending next full Master PDF/DOCX reissue  
**Authority:** Founder product direction following whole-platform review and controlled testing  
**Applies to:** Volumes 0, I, II, III, III-B, IV, V, VI, VII, the Cross-Reference Index, public/customer surfaces, and provider interoperability

## 1. Constitutional Product Rule

Furlong shall present one continuing customer relationship rather than exposing its internal module architecture. The canonical customer journey is:

**Explore → Understand → Prepare → Finance → Operate.**

The implementation may remain modular underneath, but a customer must be able to understand what Furlong sees, what remains unknown, what could change the answer, what to do next, and what has or has not been shared without learning Furlong's internal governance vocabulary.

The customer-facing positioning is outcome-first and non-determinative: **see what works before you buy or apply.** Furlong may organize verified property facts, supported interpretations, screening economics, constraints, program paths worth testing, and next actions. It may not promise property value, credit approval, program eligibility, lender acceptance, environmental clearance, or an official determination.

## 2. Customer Experience Rule — UX-GOV-001 Extension

Borrower-facing workflows shall use progressive disclosure and one useful next action at a time. Sensitive-data requests must explain why the information is requested, what it changes, who may see it, and whether the customer can continue without it. Internal doctrine identifiers, release-gate states, scoring machinery, and technical governance telemetry shall not be presented as the primary customer experience.

Readiness must be presented in the context of the actual property or transaction. Unsupported rankings, pseudo-precision, and confidence theater are prohibited. Unknown or conflicting evidence must remain visible as unknown or conflicting evidence.

## 3. My Furlong — Continuing Property Relationship

`/portal/borrower` is the canonical customer continuity surface and shall present **My Furlong / My Properties** rather than an internal borrower-module dashboard. A customer may explore and save public property records without an account. Anonymous return tokens may persist customer-selected public property snapshots without requiring a real name or email, with export and deletion rights preserved.

Each saved property should lead back to the property's analysis, readiness work, neutral provider comparison, and case-room preparation. The post-financing/ownership relationship may surface governed program, operating, market, equipment, grant, compliance, or refinancing intelligence only when supported by current source-governance evidence; Furlong shall not manufacture alerts merely to create engagement.

## 4. Current Economics — Borrower Core Journey

The currently activated borrower core journey is **free to the borrower** for:

- property intelligence and sourced screening;
- financing-readiness guidance;
- neutral provider-fit comparison; and
- customer-controlled case-room preparation.

Furlong may earn institutional revenue from software subscriptions, certification/participation, governed workflow infrastructure, API/integration access, and separately scoped professional services. Payment infrastructure may exist for governed institutional or professional scopes, but public borrower property/readiness/provider-fit surfaces may not require checkout.

Furlong does not sell borrower or loan data, sell leads, accept referral fees for ranking, or earn a commission merely because a recommended financing transaction closes under this activation policy.

## 5. Capital Network Neutrality — CAPITAL-NETWORK-NEUTRALITY-001

A provider may be listed publicly while detailed lending criteria are still being verified. **A provider may not be ranked or presented by Furlong as a transaction fit until its published credit box is verified.**

Provider-fit logic may use only verified transaction-relevant criteria, including program, property/asset type, geography, transaction-size bounds, and other published credit-box constraints. It must fail closed when required criteria are unverified or unsupported.

The following may never increase or decrease match score, rank, placement, or explanation:

- license or participation fee amount;
- subscription tier;
- referral, lead, success, or commission economics;
- affiliate/founder relationship;
- transaction revenue to Furlong; or
- any other economic benefit created by the recommendation.

Stable ties shall be resolved through a non-economic deterministic rule. The initial implementation uses provider name as the stable tie-break.

Every displayed match must explain why it fits from verified criteria. If no verified provider supports the requested comparison, Furlong must say so rather than manufacture a match.

## 6. Managed Handoff — CASE-ROOM-HANDOFF-001

Provider comparison sends no customer information. The customer chooses whether to proceed.

A Furlong case-room handoff is bound to:

1. one immutable package version;
2. one named provider;
3. one verified recipient/destination;
4. one stated purpose;
5. one permitted channel/data scope;
6. one time-bounded customer authorization; and
7. replay-safe access and delivery evidence.

Revocation and expiry fail closed. A provider may access only the consent-bound package and does not acquire authority to browse the customer's other Furlong records.

The provider does not need to adopt Furlong as its loan origination or underwriting system. Furlong is the governed interoperability and evidence boundary; the lender, broker, agency, or other authorized institution retains its own systems and its own decision authority.

Live external delivery remains blocked until the existing controlled-promotion, credential, security, reconciliation, and launch-authority gates are satisfied. Sandbox or simulated delivery must remain labeled as such.

## 7. AI, Credit, and Human Authority

Nothing in this amendment expands AI authority. In credit, eligibility, underwriting, adverse-action, or lender-decision contexts, the existing AI-TIER-001 and facilitation restrictions continue to control. Furlong may organize information and explain possible pathways; final lender/program decisions remain with credentialed human/institutional authorities.

## 8. Security and Privacy Minimums

The unified journey does not relax any security or privacy control. Borrower ownership, cross-borrower denial, named-provider binding, exact package-version binding, minimum-necessary access, revocation, expiry, audit lineage, malware scanning, secrets governance, IAP/identity boundaries, vulnerability gates, and incident/recovery controls remain mandatory.

No public-production certification may be inferred from source conformance alone. Browser-level end-to-end testing, recovery proof, DAST, external assurance where applicable, and the planned independent penetration/red-team assessment remain launch gates.
