## Backend-Impacting Updates

The current backend build must incorporate these updated requirements:

- `Volume_VI_Consolidation_and_Changes_Summary.md` is a standing
  change-control input. It records Volume VI as the de-duplicated consolidation
  of fifteen uploaded source-intelligence, scraper, revenue, runtime,
  integration, conformance, backend-readiness, module-readiness, and portable
  surface documents.
- Volume VI consolidation is additive to later Batch 25 amendments. Where the
  summary records earlier exported volume numbers, the later specific volume
  text remains controlling for its own amended doctrine.
- The Build Conformance & Cross-Reference Matrix is now a standing proof layer:
  every doctrine family must map to actual build artifacts and proof commands,
  and every capability must be marked Built, Built/BLOCKED, intentionally
  blocked, not applicable, or awaiting controlled promotion.
- No future module may claim that a capability exists unless the route, schema,
  migration/runtime file, conformance entry, and verification command exist or
  the capability is explicitly recorded as blocked pending controlled
  promotion.
- The governing build set now covers Volumes 0-VII and the Master Cross-Reference
  Index v22.0. Volume VI is the canonical authority for source intelligence,
  scraper governance, property discovery, revenue intelligence, runtime
  governance, module integration, public source surfaces, conformance, backend
  readiness, module readiness, and portable vertical surface alignment. Volume
  VII is the active unified conformance proof layer for doctrine-to-code
  traceability and build-control verification.
- `docs/master-volume-requirements.json` must include all 33 Volume VI doctrine
  IDs from Part 8 of the updated Cross-Reference Index.
- Public-safe source intelligence routes must exist for grants, property
  discovery, equipment, market context, and weather risk. These routes may only
  return DTO/view-model summaries and must preserve claims governance,
  redaction, classification filtering, audit logging, rate-limit posture, human
  review, production block posture, and no-live-fetch status.
- Public and customer-facing content must follow governed claim restrictions from `Furlong_Customer_Version.pdf` and `Furlong_Governance_Doctrines_Master_Series.pdf`.
- "Lender-ready" may only mean organized and complete against intake requirements or platform certifications. It must not mean approved, pre-approved, creditworthy, eligible for funding, underwriting-approved, or guaranteed acceptance.
- No report, package, application, profile, or module may claim public, external, cryptographic, or customer-verifiable report verification unless live verification infrastructure is operational.
- Verification watermarks, hashes, or report labels are not enough by themselves to claim public verification.
- AI must remain advisory only. It must not be described as approving, denying, underwriting, determining eligibility, making credit decisions, or influencing adverse action.
- Borrowers pay nothing for baseline borrower support. The free tier must not be used as a teaser, upsell trap, degraded workflow, or hidden paywall.
- Borrower portability, review, export, and transport rights must be available through governed machine-readable workflows and must not be premium, hidden, throttled, degraded, or blocked by dark patterns.
- Public content must preserve that Furlong facilitates coordination and is not a lender, investor, credit bureau, data vendor, underwriter, or decision-maker.
- Public content must not claim ownership, sale, or monetization of borrower, application, loan, or personal data.
- SOC 2 Type I posture may be referenced only accurately. SOC 2 Type II and FedRAMP must not be claimed as active, certified, authorized, complete, or in place unless those statuses are actually achieved.
- Credentialed Agency Ingestion is now governed by Volume I §3.37, Volume II §3.25, Volume III TECH-CONN-001, Volume IV OPS-CONN-002, and Volume V CANON-EXTSOURCE-001.
- AI-assisted authenticated source access may only be prepared as the bounded execution instrument of a credentialed human actor.
- Credentials must be represented as secure vault references only. Raw credentials must not be stored in code, environment variables, API payload records, shared caches, or connector output.
- Pre-session gates must confirm role authority, application scope, credential validity, credential ownership, whitelist approval, ToS attestation, license scope, baseline sync, isolation boundary, provenance envelope, anti-bulk posture, and Tier 1 advisory-only limits.
- Credentialed ingestion preparation must not transmit an external request, fetch official data, process the returned data, influence scoring, determine eligibility, underwrite, or create regulated fact.
- Invalid, expired, revoked, unwhitelisted, or isolation-failed credentialed ingestion attempts must create circuit-breaker posture and SEV-2 review evidence.
- External-source data cannot influence decisions, scores, eligibility, underwriting, or regulated notices until source trust, provenance, replay, authorization, and license boundaries are complete.
- Sovereign data controls remain active, with tribal data treated as high-control by default unless a sovereign authority grants a scoped and time-bound reclassification through the Sovereign Consent Gateway.
- Vertical modules must be represented by explicit manifests declaring id, route, audience, permissions, data dependencies, public-surface posture, production block posture, claims profile, and replay requirements.
- Public, borrower, lender, and sponsor surfaces must consume DTO/view-model layers rather than raw backend records.
- Cross-module integration must use governed event contracts, a shared case context, and a formal handoff map.
- `/api/public/*` must act as a public-safe gateway with classification filtering, claims governance, audience permissions, redaction rules, audit logging, rate-limiting posture, and public-safe response formatting.
- Every module and public surface must support kill-switch/feature-flag posture for module enablement, public surfaces, production live, external actions, payment capture, notice send, and raw document processing.
- Integration smoke coverage must include module registry, public surfaces, public claims, and cross-module replay checks.
- Master Volume conformance must be testable through `npm run verify:master-volumes`, backed by `docs/master-volume-requirements.json`.
- Every Master Volume doctrine represented in the requirement matrix must resolve to implemented, intentionally blocked, not applicable, or awaiting controlled promotion. Unknown doctrine state is not allowed.
- Per-module conformance must verify route presence, manifest completeness, permissions, claims posture, audit/replay posture, blocked actions, public DTO filtering, and cross-module handoffs.
- Whole-platform conformance must verify the governed chain from applications through promotion, including replay, classification, claims, and production-block posture.
- Runtime-state governance must define the allowed actions, blocked actions, audit requirements, replay requirements, AI restrictions, connector behavior, notice behavior, production authority scope, and escalation behavior for DEVELOPMENT, SANDBOX, SIMULATION, STAGING, INTERNAL_GOVERNED, PRODUCTION_SHADOW, PRODUCTION_LIVE, DEGRADED_OPERATION, DISCONNECTED_OPERATION, REPLAY_ONLY, EMERGENCY_GOVERNANCE, and SOVEREIGN_RESTRICTED.
- Constitutional feature activation must use declared feature metadata, staged rollout posture, emergency disablement, rollback lineage, activation auditing, replay-safe activation, and production authorization checks.
- Public representation and institutional claims must carry claim authority, scope, basis, verification status, audience, expiration, validation records, escalation events, and replay references.
- Constitutional incidents must include class, severity, detection timestamp, affected systems, governance escalation path, replay references, containment actions, and resolution state.
- Runtime configuration changes must be versioned, audited, replay-safe, rollback-supported, jurisdiction-aware, and constitutionally tagged.
- UX and disclosure surfaces must preserve disclosure visibility, workflow clarity, escalation visibility, accessibility compliance, explainability readability, human-review visibility, and constitutional warning placement while blocking deceptive urgency, hidden restrictions, buried disclosures, misleading approval indicators, and manipulative AI framing.
- Implementation traceability must map constitutional doctrine, regulatory policy, technical standard, runbook, canonical doctrine, service, module, route, API, database schema, event contract, test suite, runtime gate, deployment state, and certification state.
- Scrapers are governed connectors and must not act as autonomous truth systems, decision systems, scoring inputs, official reporting evidence, notice triggers, or external action engines.
- Scraper output must pass through the Source Ingestion Gate as candidate source evidence before operational use.
- Every scraper/source record must preserve source ID, source name, source URL, fetch timestamp, content hash, classification level, confidence score, replay reference, connector ID, jurisdiction scope, and scraper version.
- Source authority tiers must distinguish authoritative public/government sources, certified institutional/commercial sources, commercial marketplace sources, and advisory discovery sources.
- Property marketplace data remains advisory discovery intelligence and cannot become authoritative property truth, underwriting truth, official collateral determination, sovereign scoring evidence, or official reporting evidence without canonical governance and institutional validation.
- Canonical property governance must support deduplication, GIS reconciliation, parcel reconciliation, source conflict resolution, listing history preservation, replay-safe lineage, and provenance tracking.
- Public property discovery, lender opportunity, and sponsor project surfaces must use governed DTO/translation posture, claims governance, disclosure visibility, source authority visibility, replay-safe lineage, and advisory-only language.
- Required property discovery disclosures are: listings may change, marketplace data is advisory, institutional review may be required, and displayed properties are not approvals.
- Live scraping, autonomous borrower/lender recommendations, direct underwriting authority, sovereign scoring activation, official collateral certification, public verification authority, and AI-derived approval claims remain blocked until replay, provenance, connector, sovereignty, claims, and constitutional promotion gates pass.
- The canonical external source stack must preserve source authority tiering, connector certification, scraper runtime, source ingestion gates, provenance, classification, canonicalization, replay/audit, module consumption, and public translation boundaries.
- Canonical source coverage now includes CREXI, Land.com ecosystem, LandWatch, LandSearch, LoopNet, County GIS, Tax assessor, USDA, FSA, SBA, NOAA, NRCS, FEMA, Census, FRED, state grant portals, philanthropic grants, TractorHouse, Machinery Pete, Fastline, equipment auctions, commodity exchanges, weather/climate, soil/water, state licensing, and utility/infrastructure sources.
- Every external source must preserve authority classification, provenance score, replayability score, claims restrictions, jurisdiction tags, licensing restrictions, freshness posture, and live-fetch block status.
- Source failover, stale-source handling, queue health, conflict escalation, canonical entity lineage, source weighting, historical snapshots, and public DTO safety are now backend governance requirements.
- API aliases for programs, revenue opportunities, market signals, and geospatial suitability must remain review-bound and advisory, and must not claim program approval, legal permission, guaranteed revenue, underwriting reliance, lender commitment, or official collateral certification.
- Batch 25 environmental governance is now active:
  - Volume I `ROLE-ARCH-001` adds Environmental Engineering Spoke authority for
    NEPA screening, Phase I/II/III ESA routing, jurisdictional environmental
    compliance verification, Banker Spoke isolation, external firm equivalency,
    and cross-pathway exemption alignment.
  - Volume III `TECH-CONN-001` requires `environmental_compliance_records`
    with assessment type, triggering pathway, provider type, state license ref,
    assessment outcome, fee disclosure ref, audit anchor, and spoke isolation.
  - Volume IV `OPS-BORROWER-JOURNEY-001` Steps 2.5-2.7 require environmental
    trigger evaluation, provider/license verification, fee transparency, audit
    anchor registration, and lineage confirmation before pathway advancement.
  - Volume V `CANON-ECON-001` and `CANON-SOVEREIGNTY-001` require borrower fee
    disclosure, borrower freedom to select an approved external firm without
    routing penalty, Environmental Engineering Spoke discount posture, and
    state-level environmental provider credential verification.
