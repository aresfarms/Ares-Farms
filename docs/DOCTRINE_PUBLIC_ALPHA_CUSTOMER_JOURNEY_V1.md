# Build 38 — Public Alpha Profile v1

## Purpose

Build 38 establishes the canonical Public Alpha Profile for Furlong.

The purpose of Public Alpha is not full public launch. The purpose is to validate the customer journey, trust model, disclosure model, human authority model, and customer-facing experience using the governed platform stack already implemented through Modules 1–45.

Public Alpha represents the first controlled exposure of the platform to real users.

---

# Why This Build Exists

Modules 34, 44, and 45 completed the foundational governance requirements necessary for customer-facing operation:

- Build Self-Report Runtime
- Disclosure Audit Gate
- Human Authority Registry

The platform can now determine:

- who has authority
- what may be disclosed
- what may not be claimed
- which decisions require human involvement
- which modules are intentionally held

The remaining challenge is customer experience.

Public Alpha exists to validate how users interact with the platform.

---

# Public Alpha Objectives

Validate:

1. Customer understanding
2. Trust generation
3. Recommendation clarity
4. Human escalation paths
5. Data transparency
6. Disclosure visibility
7. Customer journey completion

---

# Public Alpha Customer Promise

Furlong is a Compass to Capital.

Furlong does not:

- approve loans
- deny loans
- issue underwriting decisions
- issue agency determinations
- guarantee funding

Furlong helps users understand:

- available pathways
- readiness gaps
- documentation needs
- financing realities
- environmental considerations
- next recommended actions

before significant time, money, or effort are committed.

---

# Public Alpha Entry Surface

The first customer-facing experience shall include:

## 1. Founder Introduction

Primary founder video and/or written introduction.

Purpose:

- establish trust
- explain mission
- explain platform limitations

---

## 2. Customer Project Intake

Simple guided intake.

Examples:

- What type of business do you own?
- What are you trying to accomplish?
- Where is the property located?
- What type of asset is involved?

---

## 3. Pathway Discovery

Present:

- likely pathways
- excluded pathways
- rationale

No approval language permitted.

---

## 4. Readiness Review

Present:

- readiness indicators
- missing items
- documentation recommendations

---

## 5. Financing Reality Classification

Present:

- likely financeable
- financeable with conditions
- specialist review required
- limited financing market
- cash-favored transaction
- not enough information

---

## 6. Human Escalation

Present:

- available human review paths
- authority assignments
- next-step guidance

---

## 7. Data Transparency

Present:

- what information is collected
- why information is collected
- how information is used
- what has been shared
- what has not been shared

Must be visible without requiring legal-document review.

---

# Alpha Success Criteria

Public Alpha succeeds when a customer can answer:

1. What are my options?
2. What are my risks?
3. What documents do I need?
4. What happens to my data?
5. What should I do next?
6. Who can help me?

without requiring external explanation.

---

# Constitutional Posture

Public Alpha remains:

- advisory only
- non-lending
- non-underwriting
- non-regulatory
- non-determinative

All disclosure requirements from Module 44 remain mandatory.

All authority requirements from Module 45 remain mandatory.

---

# Definition of Done

1. Public Alpha Profile documented.
2. Alpha-required roles assigned.
3. Disclosure Audit Gate PASS.
4. Human Authority Registry PASS.
5. Build Self-Report exits 0 for Alpha-required modules.
6. Customer journey validated through smoke testing.
7. Customer-facing entry surface available for controlled testing.

Public Alpha approval requires named human authority and successful governance verification.
