# Governance Exception Registry

Per VIA-AUDIT-005. Undocumented exceptions are prohibited; every exception is documented, time-bounded, reviewable, and compensating-control protected.

---

## VIA-AUDIT-EXCEPTION-001 — Public Alpha Internal Verification Exception

**Status:** Active

### Description
During Development and Public Alpha, the Technical Infrastructure Steward performs internal verification of controls, build procedures, conformance checks, and audit artifacts due to the absence of an appointed Independent Verification Authority.

### Exception Scope
**Applies only to:**
- Public Alpha
- Development environments
- Internal governance review

**Does not apply to:**
- Production Certification
- External audit attestation
- Regulatory reliance
- Investor certification
- Public verification claims

### Compensating Control
Prior to Production Certification:
- An Independent Verification Authority shall be appointed.
- Verification procedures shall be re-run independently.
- Results shall be separately attested.

### Attribution
- **Approved by:** Owner-controlled governance transition; independent review remains required before production certification
- **Effective date:** _[recorded at ceremony]_
- **Exception Owner:** Governance Authority
- **Review owner:** Independent Governance Reviewer (appointment pending)
- **Review cadence:** at each material governance review and prior to Production Certification

### Time Bound
Effective at Public Alpha entry. **Remains active until the first independent attestation by an appointed Independent Verification Authority is recorded** (not merely IVA appointment), or Production Certification, whichever is earlier.
