Data Retention and Disposal Policy

Policy version: 1.1 · Effective and last reviewed: August 8, 2026 · Next scheduled review: August 8, 2027, or sooner on material change.

Core rule: Personal and consumer financial data is retained only for an active, disclosed purpose and any documented legal, regulatory, dispute, fraud, security, forensic, or audit hold. Indefinite retention is prohibited.

Plaid data

Plaid access tokens and Plaid-derived consumer data may be persisted only through Furlong's ciphertext-only Plaid secure store. Access tokens are never written to plaintext database columns, logs, browser storage, or audit payloads.

When account permission is withdrawn, a linked account is disconnected, or the financing purpose no longer requires access, Furlong revokes provider access and cryptographically purges the local encrypted records unless a documented hold applies.

Other data classes

Uploaded documents, signature evidence, identity-verification evidence, transaction provenance, privileged-access evidence, security telemetry, and audit evidence are retained only for their disclosed operational or lawful purpose. When eligible, Furlong deletes underlying content or cryptographically renders it unrecoverable while preserving only minimum-necessary process evidence.

Consumer rights

Consumers may request access, export, correction, restriction, deletion, or human review. Requests affecting regulated, signed, disputed, lender-relied-upon, or legally significant records are human-reviewed before destruction.

Review and accountability

This policy is reviewed at least annually and whenever a material legal, provider, product, security, or data-practice change occurs. Retention changes must be reflected in executable retention rules, consent language, data-rights procedures, and provider disconnect/deletion flows.
