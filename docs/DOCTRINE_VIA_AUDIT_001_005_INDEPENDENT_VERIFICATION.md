# Independent Verification Doctrine — v1

**Volume:** VI-A (Founder Governance & Institutional Stewardship) — Audit Independence amendment.
**Status:** Ratified. Generated from ratified conversation text 2026-06-04. Role-holder names live in the Volume VII Operational Annex.

---

## VIA-AUDIT-001 — Principle

Independent verification requires separation between:

- Control Builder
- Control Reviewer

A person may build a control. A person may verify a control. **The same person shall not serve as both builder and independent verifier for production certification.**

---

## VIA-AUDIT-002 — Independent Verification Authority

The institution shall maintain an Independent Verification Authority (IVA).

**Requirements.** The authority:

- Must not be the primary implementer of the reviewed control.
- Must have sufficient access to reproduce verification procedures.
- Must document findings.
- Must record attestation results.

---

## VIA-AUDIT-003 — Public Alpha Exception

During Development and Public Alpha, internal verification may be performed by the Technical Infrastructure Steward when an Independent Verification Authority has not yet been appointed.

All such results shall be labeled: **"Internally Verified — Independent Verification Pending."**

---

## VIA-AUDIT-004 — Production Certification Requirement

Production Certification may not rely solely upon internally verified controls. At least one independent verifier shall:

- Re-run verification procedures
- Review evidence
- Review outputs
- Record attestation

before production certification may be granted.

---

## VIA-AUDIT-005 — Governance Exception Registry

Where separation-of-duty requirements cannot be satisfied due to institutional size, the exception shall be:

- documented
- time-bounded
- reviewable
- compensating-control protected

**Undocumented exceptions are prohibited.** (See the Governance Exception Registry; first entry: VIA-AUDIT-EXCEPTION-001.)
