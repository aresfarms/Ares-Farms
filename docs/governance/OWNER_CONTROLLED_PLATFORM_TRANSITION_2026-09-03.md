# Owner-Controlled Platform Transition - 2026-09-03

**Status:** Effective for current platform architecture and governance configuration.
**Owner/operator:** Caitlin Hudson.
**Scope:** Furlong platform governance, operational authority, stewardship, professional-spoke boundaries, and the commercial-finance expansion roadmap.

## 1. Current-control rule

Furlong is operated as an owner-controlled platform. No former founder, steward, reviewer, communications authority, capital authority, or partner identity is required for Furlong to operate, develop, test, or continue its governed build.

This transition changes current platform authority and dependencies. It does **not** by itself adjudicate or modify any separate executed corporate equity, contract, intellectual-property, compensation, or third-party agreement; those instruments require their own legally effective action if any exist.

## 2. External broker workspace exception

The existing broker Deal Desk may remain accessible to its current external broker operator during transition. That access is an isolated professional-spoke workspace only.

The workspace grants no Furlong ownership, founder status, governance vote, source-legal approval, public-trust authority, treasury authority, release authority, audit authority, or right to direct platform architecture. It may be disabled later without changing Furlong Core.

## 3. Current authority posture

- Platform ownership and day-to-day operating authority: Caitlin Hudson.
- Financing and capital stewardship surface: role-based **Furlong Capital Desk**.
- Communications and public-trust stewardship surface: role-based **Furlong Trust Desk**.
- Final credit and program eligibility authority: external lender or agency, never Furlong AI.
- Independent governance/control verification: appointment pending; constitutionally significant self-approval remains prohibited and must fail closed until a qualified independent reviewer is identity-bound.
- External professional spokes: optional, replaceable, independently governed, and never ownership or governance authorities merely because they have portal access.

## 4. Commercial-finance pivot - approved sequence

1. Remove personal ownership/governance dependencies while retaining the isolated broker workspace temporarily.
2. Operate Furlong as the borrower-intake, commercial-finance packaging, program-navigation, document-readiness, environmental-coordination, and lender-routing platform, subject to applicable licensing and compensation rules.
3. Build a diversified lender network so no single broker or lender is a platform dependency.
4. Establish any future lending activity in a separately capitalized and separately governed lending affiliate rather than collapsing lender authority into Furlong Core.
5. Pursue lender approvals program-by-program after the affiliate has the capital, operating history, qualified staff, policies, servicing capability, and regulatory approvals required for the target program.

## 5. Economic and routing conflict rule

If a Furlong-affiliated lender is later created, it is only one financing spoke. Furlong may not rank, route, recommend, or suppress options merely to favor an affiliated economic interest. Any affiliated-lender participation must be disclosed and conflict-governed, and the borrower must remain free to select another qualified provider.

## 6. Historical evidence rule

Immutable audit artifacts, deployment records, signed attestations, executed agreements, and historical build records are not rewritten to erase who actually acted at the time. They may retain historical names solely as evidence. Current authority registries and public/current operational surfaces must not infer continuing authority from those records.

## 7. Master Volume Series status

The current Master Volume Series is institution- and role-oriented. Personal partner names are not required to state Furlong doctrine. Current canonical volumes should remain person-neutral; operational roster changes belong in the current authority/transition layer rather than being embedded as permanent constitutional identity.
