# Furlong: small organizational pilot operating plan
Date: 2026-09-08 | Status: owner operating plan, not a signed offer or revenue forecast.

## Product being sold

Furlong helps customers understand a property/project, organize supported evidence and unresolved questions, and coordinate chosen professional next steps. It is not a listings marketplace, lender approval system or engineering-only platform.

Keep core customer exploration and case coordination free. Offer organizations a defined workflow service only when it actually saves work in their repeated case type. Separately commissioned professional services remain optional. Neither payment nor affiliation can change program/provider ranking, access to financing or customer disclosure permissions.

## Practical next sequence

1. Complete the affected calculation, evidence, export and security release gates. Demonstrate only verified capabilities; keep unsupported outcomes pending.
2. Use an owner-approved test case with no private borrower material for initial demonstrations. Ask a prospective organization to walk through the package, identify missing evidence and name the next person responsible. Record what they would use again.
3. Invite a small, capacity-limited group only after its permitted testing scope is clear. No prospect messages or invitations have been sent by this implementation.
4. Before accepting money, agree in writing on deliverables, permitted users, support hours, response windows, acceptance criteria, term, cancellation/refund handling, data handling and price. Route actual receipts through existing treasury controls. Do not promise unlimited founder access.
5. Review at a fixed weekly operator session; reconcile payments and direct costs monthly. Expand only after useful repeat use, actual paid renewal and sustainable delivery effort. Do not infer willingness to pay from compliments, a demonstration or traffic.

## Measurement contract

No current baseline, paid institutional revenue, conversion rate or renewal rate is asserted. The following are forward definitions, not a newly instrumented dashboard. Owner maintains the pilot register and reconciles it to signed scopes, paid invoices/refunds, actual expenses and time records. Missing source records mean “not measured,” never zero. Do not log raw public conversations for marketing measurement.

| Metric | Definition and grain | Evidence and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Net operating cash contribution | Monthly cash received for delivered service periods, less refunds, direct delivery/support costs and allocated platform cash expenses. Separate advances and refundable deposits; exclude loan proceeds. | Reconcile payment/refund records and invoices to treasury. Report founder hours separately and also show contribution after hours valued at the owner's stated $250/hour reference rate. This is an operating measure, not a tax/accounting profit determination. Do not discount services without measuring delivery effort. |
| Paid organizational renewal | Organizations renewing and paying divided by organizations whose paid term actually ended and were eligible to renew, monthly cohort. If none due, not applicable. | Signed scope + paid renewal + term dates. Distinguish setup fees, first payment and renewal. Count organizations, not seats, invoices or free extensions. If users return but buyers do not pay, revisit the offer rather than claiming recurring demand. |
| Useful task completion | Observed pilot tasks in which the customer can explain supported facts, material unknowns and the next action without operator rescue, divided by observed eligible attempts. A supported stop/no-go can count; a saved file alone does not. | Consented test-session checklist with outcome, failure reason and support minutes; no private transcript required. Report sample counts and assisted vs unassisted results. Small convenience samples do not establish a population conversion rate. |

Guardrails: (1) any cross-owner disclosure or unsupported material finance/property claim pauses the affected workflow and starts incident/correction handling; (2) record founder delivery and support minutes per paid organization and per useful task, including free presales work. If delivery exceeds the agreed allowance, fix the workflow or rescope future terms; do not silently charge extra.

Drivers: number of eligible organizations completing a second real case and support minutes required to do so. Page visits and signups are diagnostic only, not the success definition.

The owner's $10,000/month initial income aim and $20,000–$50,000 later aspiration are planning goals, not supported forecasts. Establish actual price, hours and contribution before calculating the number of organizations needed. Distinguish personal compensation from revenue and operating contribution.

## Scope boundaries and unresolved work

No new subscription processor, fixed price, paid contract or automated marketing surveillance is activated. A future billing implementation requires an actual offer, delivery capacity and the existing scope/fee/treasury controls. Exact-recipient case-room authorization remains the only platform-managed sharing path; an owner personal download grants no provider access.

Guides have source-check dates, not an assurance that all source pages or program terms stay current. Review them when cited official material changes and before using them in a paid deliverable. The agricultural guide must never turn a general soil-map reference into a parcel pH or crop recommendation.
