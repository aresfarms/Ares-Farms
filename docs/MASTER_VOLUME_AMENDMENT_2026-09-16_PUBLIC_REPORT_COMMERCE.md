**Authority:** Founder-approved public product and commercial direction  
**Applies to:** Public property intelligence, report products, pricing, checkout,

related provisions of Volumes 0, I, II, III, III-B, IV, V, VI, and VII.

## 1. Scope and precedence

This amendment establishes the authorized public property-report ladder. Within
that scope, it supersedes the September 5 and September 8 wording that made all
customer property intelligence a permanently free core. It refines the
September 8 Property Decision and Annual Analysis authorization into the two
specific paid products below.

The free financing-access rules remain intact. Furlong may not charge for loan
approval, lender access, provider rank, favorable treatment, case sharing, or
an institutional decision. Provider neutrality, consent, facilitation, privacy,
fair-lending, and exact-recipient controls are not relaxed.

Historical Master PDFs remain unchanged until the next controlled reissue. This
amendment is product doctrine and an implementation record; it is not legal
advice, a professional certification, proof of cloud deployment, or proof of
market adoption.

## 2. Public product ladder

| Product                          | Authorized customer job                                        |       Proposed price | Fulfillment               |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------: | ------------------------- |
| Furlong Property Snapshot        | Decide whether more investigation is useful                    |                   $0 | Immediate screen          |
| Furlong Property Report          | Understand one property and its strongest supported uses       |             $49 once | Automated, version frozen |
| Furlong Property Decision Report | Decide whether, at what price, and under which plan to proceed | $249 base scope once | Human reviewed            |

There is no customer-facing third or overlapping middle report. The legacy
Complete Property Analysis placeholder is retired and may not be sold.

The $49 and $249 amounts are testable price hypotheses, not permanent prices
and not authorization to activate checkout. Each requires current direct cost,
support burden, refund exposure, payment fees, margin, and willingness-to-pay
evidence for the exact catalog version.

No monthly public subscription is authorized. Quarterly, six-month, annual, or
high-frequency access may be designed later only after fair-use limits, batch
costs, entitlement behavior, name, and price are separately approved.

## 3. Free Property Snapshot

The free Snapshot must remain genuinely useful. It includes matched property
identity and current-use context, material constraints and known warnings,
source coverage, confidence, missing evidence, and a specific next action.

It need not include complete enterprise economics, long-range projections, a
human-reviewed acquisition decision, or a downloadable paid artifact. A free
screen may recommend no further purchase when the supported answer is already
clear.

## 4. Automated Property Report

The Property Report includes automated property, parcel, use, market, and
constraint analysis; a preliminary comparison of strongest single use, strongest
mixed use, and the customer's selected feasible vision; preliminary NOI, DSCR,
and projections when supported inputs exist; financing and incentive families;
sources, assumptions, evidence gaps; and a downloadable version-frozen report.

It does not include human review, a negotiated acquisition recommendation,
borrower underwriting, appraisal, loan approval, a guaranteed outcome, site
visits, sampling, laboratory work, stamped plans, or an official professional
report.

Payment may not be taken until the exact server-generated artifact exists with
an immutable reference, SHA-256 digest, generation time, source lineage, and
catalog/schema versions. The order, entitlement, and delivered artifact must
remain bound to that exact version. Missing artifact proof fails closed before
payment.

## 5. Property Decision Report

The Property Decision Report is a human-reviewed decision product. It includes
ranked single-enterprise, mixed-use, and selected-vision scenarios; acquisition
price and offer boundaries; sensitivity testing; supportable monthly, quarterly,
annual, 5-, 10-, and 30-year projections; labor, benefits, inflation,
maintenance, replacement, NOI, and DSCR analysis; competition; financing and
grant pathways; execution conditions; and an explicit action verdict.

The $249 amount covers only the displayed base scope. It does not include
borrower personal-financial underwriting, lender commitment, appraisal, legal
advice, permit or agency determination, guaranteed profit, or separately scoped
professional field work.

The final Decision Report PDF must upload directly to governed private object
storage, pass exact byte-count and SHA-256 verification, malware scanning, and
PDF structural-safety checks, and remain bound to the paid order before an
operator may complete fulfillment. The customer receives only an order-token or
owning-session portal download; the private object reference is never exposed.
Every download is an immutable order event, and a refund or dispute blocks the
download route before report bytes are returned.

## 6. Neutral recommendation and scenario order

Furlong must recommend the smallest sufficient product from evidence and
decision need, not the product producing the most revenue. A valid result is
**Do not buy this report yet.**

Every property evaluation preserves three candidates when supportable:

1. the strongest supported single-enterprise use;
2. the strongest supported mixed-use combination; and
3. the customer's selected feasible vision.

The three are ranked by the same disclosed evidence model. If the customer's
vision ranks first, it moves to first place; the other two remain visible as
financing or execution alternatives. Missing evidence, exclusions, walk-away
conditions, and uncertainty may not be hidden to induce a sale.

The public interface offers only property-supported vision choices. Unlisted or
novel concepts go through separate custom-concept review; unrestricted joke,
impossible, or abusive free-text visions do not enter the automated ranking.

## 7. One-time upgrade credit

A fulfilled, fully paid, non-refunded, non-disputed $49 Property Report may
receive one $49 credit toward the $249 base Property Decision Report for the
same property when requested within 30 days of payment.

The source order must be privately authorized, same-property verified, unused,
and atomically reserved. The customer sees list price, credit, and final amount
before accepting. Different-property, expired, reused, unpaid, refunded,
disputed, or unauthorized orders receive no credit and leak no source details.
An unpaid failed or expired target checkout releases its reservation without
creating a second credit. A target order fully refunded before processing also
releases the source credit while retaining the historical price and credit
evidence on the refunded order. If the source payment is later refunded or
disputed, the credited target is held, its access is revoked, and payment or
fulfillment cannot resume until an operator resolves the missing value.

## 8. Professional-services separation

Phase I, II, and III work, remediation, site supervision, sampling, laboratory
work, environmental/civil/chemical/nuclear/general engineering consultation,
professional-firm referral, and custom-concept review are separately scoped
professional services. The public intake requests scope only: it takes no
payment and makes no universal delivery-time promise.

Furlong may perform work it is qualified and authorized to perform under a
separate written scope. Furlong does not stamp drawings and does not consult on
structural or electrical plans; work requiring stamped plans or those
disciplines is referred to an appropriate firm. A Property Report or Decision
Report purchase never silently orders professional field work.

## 9. Financing and incentive boundary

Furlong evaluates the property and proposed enterprise, organizes project-side
evidence, tests financing and program families, identifies grants and
incentives, prepares and routes authorized paper, tracks conditions, and may
coordinate the transaction. It does not become the lender, broker, agency,
appraiser, or final decision-maker by doing so.

The public property-report path does not require SSN, personal bank balance, or
personal credit data. Property financeability is not borrower approval. The
lender or program authority retains underwriting, eligibility, commitment,
adverse-action, and closing authority.

Payment may never buy lender access, provider placement, rank, eligibility, a
better recommendation, or an outcome. Lead sales, hidden referral economics,
success percentages tied to funded amount, and sale of customer information
remain prohibited.

## 10. Payment, refund, and dispute rule

Before payment, the customer must see the exact property, product, included and
excluded scope, delivery promise, list price, any credit, and final amount and
must affirmatively accept the current short purchase terms.

For a supervised Decision Report, a full cancellation/refund is available
before operator processing begins. Automated processing begins after signed
payment confirmation and artifact verification. After processing starts or a
report is delivered, an unfavorable answer, financing denial, or change of mind
does not by itself create a refund right. Duplicate charges, an unfulfillable
order, or a materially missing promised section require correction or refund
under the published terms and applicable law.

A dispute immediately revokes report access while retaining the event, order,
artifact, agreement, and delivery evidence. Refund and dispute status must be
reconciled only from signed processor events. No `ZERO RETURNS` slogan or
contract language overrides applicable law or a promised-product failure.

## 11. Release and evidence gates

Both paid products remain fail closed until their separate sales flag is
enabled. Activation additionally requires an approved current price review,
signed Stripe webhook coverage, immutable ledger/event behavior, tested
cancellation/refund/dispute flows, entitlement isolation, retained replay
evidence, security gates, and a verified deployed revision.

The automated Property Report additionally requires the artifact generation and
immutable delivery path. The Decision Report additionally requires a definite
delivery window, positive supervised capacity, and deployed proof of its
verified private-artifact upload, completion, customer download, and revocation
path. Passing source tests alone is not evidence that either product works in
Google Cloud.

Source implementation exists for the product catalog, neutral recommendation,
checkout agreement, supervised order controls, single-use upgrade credit,
professional-services scoping, independent kill switches, the explicit
pre-payment automated-artifact block, and the supervised Decision Report's

amendment does not claim that migration 0065 is promoted, that the new artifact
path has passed deployed browser testing, or that either product is customer
ready.

The $49 product remains blocked on the real immutable artifact worker/delivery
integration and paid entitlement proof. The $249 Decision Report has its source
delivery path but remains blocked on deployed end-to-end proof. Both products
remain blocked on current price validation, complete Stripe test/live-webhook
evidence, refund/dispute operations, deployed browser testing, legal review,
and security/promotion evidence. Both sales flags and all paid-order capacities
therefore remain off/zero.

## 14. Final hard rule

Furlong may charge for deeper analysis and human work, but it may not charge
before it can deliver the exact promised artifact safely and repeatably. The
customer gets the smallest sufficient product, the truth even when the answer
is to walk away, visible evidence and uncertainty, neutral financing pathways,
and a clean boundary between a report and separately scoped professional work.
