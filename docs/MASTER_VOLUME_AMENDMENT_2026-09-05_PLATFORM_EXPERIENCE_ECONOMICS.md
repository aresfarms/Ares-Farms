**Applies to:** Furlong public experience, property intelligence, financing readiness, Capital Network, customer case continuity, institutional economics, outcome learning, and production-security claims.

## 1. Category position and customer job

Furlong is the independent **property-to-capital intelligence and coordination layer** between a property idea and the capital required to make it real. This is a customer-facing category position; it does not replace the constitutional identity of Furlong as governed interoperability infrastructure.

The primary customer job is not “find a loan.” It is: **understand the place, determine whether the project makes sense, identify what can stop it, determine the capital path, and carry one governed case through the named institutional handoff to keys.**

The product sequence is therefore:

**Property → Feasibility → Financing Readiness → Verified Provider Comparison → Exact-Recipient Case Room → Diligence → Closing → Keys → Operating Logbook.**

## 2. Customer-experience hard rule

Every material decision surface follows progressive disclosure in this order:

1. **What did Furlong find?**
2. **Why does it matter?**
3. **What should the customer do next?**
4. **Show me why:** evidence, sources, assumptions, conflicts, unknowns, methods, and technical/governance detail.

Governance detail remains fully available but may not crowd out the customer decision on the first screen. Public copy uses customer language rather than internal module names where an internal noun would require explanation.

An unsupported answer is not improved by confident presentation. Recommendation text, ranking, economics, financing indication, and supporting evidence must reconcile before a leading use or financed coverage result is shown. A valid Furlong answer may be **do not buy / do not proceed yet** when the supported evidence indicates that result.

## 3. Customer-free financing core

The following are a standing customer-free core and may not be gated by a borrower financing-access fee:

- open property analysis;
- financing-readiness organization;
- verified-provider comparison;
- customer-controlled case continuity;
- exact-recipient, expiring case-room handoff;
- status coordination through lender response, conditions, diligence, closing, and keys/logbook.

The funding institution remains responsible for its own loan pricing, fees, underwriting, approval, commitment, adverse action, servicing, and program determinations.

Optional professional or archival work may be separately scoped when lawful and appropriately credentialed. That optional work may never purchase financing access, eligibility, provider placement, faster credit treatment, a better rank, or a different Furlong recommendation.

## 4. Institutional economics and prohibited monetization

Furlong is funded primarily through **institutional infrastructure economics**, including enterprise licensing, institutional subscriptions, workflow infrastructure, API and integration access, case-room/document-normalization infrastructure, analytics, governance/audit tooling, support/SLAs, and enterprise deployment environments.

A verified institution may qualify for a public profile or match because it satisfies the governed participation and evidence requirements; **payment never buys inclusion, placement, rank, a lead, or customer-file access.**

The following monetization patterns are prohibited:

- borrower lead sale;
- borrower-file auction;
- shotgun or automatic provider broadcast;
- provider pay-to-rank or paid preferred placement;
- hidden referral economics;
- referral fee tied to the borrower’s financing outcome;
- Furlong success percentage or transaction percentage based on funded amount;
- sale of customer information.

Provider compensation or affiliation has zero influence on match score or provider rank.

## 5. Canonical living Furlong Case

A Furlong Case is a durable, customer-controlled record carrying the same matter across property analysis, feasibility, readiness, environmental work, capital coordination, permissions, providers, closing, and later operating memory.

The canonical case contains, as applicable:

- property identity and property snapshot;
- customer goal and business context;
- borrower-readiness evidence and missing-item state;
- environmental context and professional-review lineage;
- capital-path context;
- document references;
- permission / consent state;
- provider selections and case-room state;
- append-oriented case events;
- verified actual outcomes;
- later operating-logbook continuity.

Saving a case does **not** disclose it to a provider. Provider sharing remains exact-recipient, exact-package, purpose-bound, channel-bound, separately consented, expiring, and revocable.

## 6. Verified provider published-box evidence

The Capital Network preserves provider-published program/deal criteria, collateral posture, environmental requirements, geographic/program appetite, deal-size bounds, and provider-published response/closing expectations together with source references and verification time.

Furlong must distinguish:

- provider-declared appetite;
- **source-verified published provider box**;
- provider-published turnaround expectations; and
- Furlong-measured execution history from verified cases.

Those concepts may never be collapsed. A provider’s published expectation is not Furlong-measured performance.

For current nonresidential matching, published-box evidence improves the explanation and supports deterministic deal/property/program fit. Personal credit and person-side eligibility remain within the provider’s authorized underwriting process and do not become a Furlong AI credit decision. Administrative verification status itself may not create a ranking advantage.

## 7. Managed provider handoff

A customer may privately compare a bounded number of supported providers. Every provider is its own case-room/consent boundary.

Required sequence:

**one readiness file → verified provider fit → customer chooses recipient(s) → frozen package → separate exact-recipient consent → verified recipient authority → expiring case room → provider structured response → conditions/diligence → closing → keys/logbook.**

Provider structured responses include: accept for review, missing information, outside credit box, conditional path, or decline. The provider may continue using its own LOS or institutional systems; Furlong is not required to become the lender’s loan system.

## 8. Actual outcome learning

Furlong may preserve evidence-backed actual outcomes including:

- submission time;
- provider first response/disposition;
- accept/decline/withdrawal/block reason category;
- conditions;
- financing structure;
- actual rate where provided and authorized;
- actual project cost where verified;
- environmental outcome;
- closing time / closed-funded state.

Outcome learning is evidence-backed and versioned. It may not become hidden autonomous credit authority. Provider execution metrics remain subject to minimum verified sample thresholds, and borrower/external exits remain separated from provider-decision performance as already required by the Capital Network execution-reliability doctrine.

## 9. Market specialization sequence

The initial depth/coverage sequence is a **product-development and provider-network priority**, not an eligibility hierarchy:

1. USDA Business & Industry (B&I);
2. SBA 504;
3. SBA 7(a);
4. FSA Farm Ownership and Operating;
5. USDA Community Facilities and REAP;
6. conventional commercial;
7. residential conventional only where the property journey requires it.

A customer whose supported facts fit a later-listed pathway must still receive that pathway. Product sequencing may never suppress a better-supported customer result.

## 10. Security assurance and production claim rule

Furlong’s constitutional security architecture is necessary but is not, by itself, evidence that an unrestricted production deployment is secure enough.

Unrestricted production activation for the affected live actions requires current evidence for:

- threat-model closure;
- dependency and container/image scanning;
- IAM and least-privilege review;
- tenant/case-room authorization and isolation testing;
- KMS, key, credential, and secret rotation evidence;
- backup, restore, immutable-ledger, and replay exercises;
- penetration/security testing;
- incident, rollback, kill-switch, and recovery exercises;
- vendor/provider security and data-boundary review;
- signed governed promotion gates and deployed verification.

Missing evidence keeps the affected action blocked. Documentation may state **architecture implemented** or **source verified** where supported; it may not state unrestricted production assurance until the evidence gate passes.

## 13. Final hard rule

Furlong may be sophisticated internally while remaining simple to the customer. The customer receives the answer first, the evidence on demand, a neutral capital path, control over every recipient, and a case that survives the handoffs. Revenue may fund the infrastructure; it may not purchase the customer’s outcome.
